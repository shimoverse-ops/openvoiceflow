# Support

OpenVoiceFlow is a single-maintainer source-available project. Response time is best-effort. The fastest way to make progress on something is usually to debug it yourself and send a small PR with a failing test.

[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Found a bug?

Open a GitHub Issue: <https://github.com/shimoverse/openvoiceflow/issues/new>.

Include:
- Your macOS version (`sw_vers -productVersion`)
- Your Mac arch (`uname -m`)
- Your app version (Dashboard → Settings shows it)
- Which speech engine you chose, and what the HUD showed when it went wrong
- What you expected vs what happened
- The shortest reproduction you can come up with

## Got a question or want to brainstorm a feature?

Use GitHub Discussions: <https://github.com/shimoverse/openvoiceflow/discussions>. That's the right place for "how do I…?", "would you accept a PR that…?", or "I'm thinking about adding X — thoughts?"

## Security concern?

**Don't open a public issue.** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting channel.

## Privacy or compliance question?

See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) and [docs/COMPLIANCE.md](docs/COMPLIANCE.md). For questions not covered there, open a Discussion.
